# Getting through exam season

Dev fixture body. Revision weeks can make everything feel urgent at once, and
that urgency is tiring in itself. None of this is about working harder — it's
about making the weeks feel lighter to carry.

## Make the work smaller

1. **Break it into small blocks.** Twenty-five minutes on, five off.
2. **Keep one thing that isn't revision.** A walk, a call, a meal with someone.
3. **Sleep counts as study.** Memory settles overnight.

> [!TIP]
> Write tomorrow's first task down before you stop tonight. Starting is the
> hardest part, and you've already done it.

## When it all piles up

Panic tends to arrive as *everything at once*. Pick the next single thing and
let the rest wait its turn. If the worry stops you sleeping or eating for more
than a couple of weeks, it's worth telling someone you trust.

---

Every exam season ends. The version of you on the other side will be glad you
were gentle with this one.
