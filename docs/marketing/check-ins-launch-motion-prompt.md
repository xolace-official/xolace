## 0. Your role

You are the best motion designer in the world. You are leading the launch film for a
top-tier, trillion-dollar company: the kind of short, quiet product film where the real
product, shown big and clean on a beautiful background, is the hero. Restraint is the
standard. One idea, carried completely, with nothing on screen that doesn't earn its
place.

Treat this brief as the client's truth about the product. Where it sets a rule, follow it
exactly. Where it describes a direction, beat it if you can, and say why.

This is the second film in a series about Xolace's new features. The first was about
Letters. They share palette, type, sound and end card.

## 1. The product, in one minute

**Xolace** is a mental-health companion app for iOS and Android. People talk or write
through how they feel and keep a gentle daily rhythm.

When a conversation with Xolace ends with something still unsettled, Xolace **checks in**
a while later: *"A little while ago you let something out here. How's it sitting now?"*

Until now, that check-in waited inside the app, and on a busy day most people never open
it. Now it comes to an app people already have open all day. The first is **Telegram**;
more will follow.

- You link Telegram once, in the app, under **Reach me on…**.
- A check-in arrives as a short message with three answers: **🌤 Lighter**,
  **☁️ Still with it**, **🌧 Heavier**. One tap answers it, without opening Xolace.
- Or you **write back** in your own words, and Xolace replies, briefly.
- Once answered, the message closes to "✓ Checked in". Done.
- Quiet hours apply: it won't buzz you at night.

### The benefit (what the film is really about)

**Someone remembered to ask, and answering took a second.** Hours after a hard moment,
Xolace asks how it's sitting, right in the chat app you already have open. You don't have
to remember, find or open anything. One tap, and you've noticed how you're doing.

The feeling to leave: *it found me, it was easy, and it felt good to be asked.*

## 2. Notes on the last test (v2)

**Keep:** the lavender overcast sky, the clear sky at the end (blue top, warm horizon),
the dark phone on a light sky, the Newsreader Italic supers, the calm pace.

**Drop, and don't bring back:**

- Placeholder bars or any invented interface. If it's on a phone screen, it's real
  footage.
- The dotted path, the grids of grey icon squares and the home-screen pages.
- Cropping the phone at the frame edge. The phone is always whole and inside frame.
- Big empty areas filled with a blurred blob. If a side of the frame is empty, it's open
  sky with a line of type in it, or the phone moves to balance it.
- Several push-ins and slides of the same phone. The phone moves in once, holds, and
  moves out once.

## 3. Craft rules for this film

- **No human figures.** No people, faces, bodies, silhouettes, hands or arms, no animals,
  no characters. The only trace of a person is a **touch dot**: a soft translucent circle
  (about 9% of the phone's width) where a tap happens.
- **Two things on screen, ever:** the sky, and the phone playing real footage. Plus one
  line of type at a time. Nothing else: no icons floating, no stickers, no weather cards,
  no particles, no rain.
- **One focal point at a time.** While the phone is on screen, the eye is on the phone.
- **Readable on a phone.** In 16:9 the phone is about 82% of frame height. In 9:16 it is
  about 64% of frame height. The check-in text must be legible at those sizes; if it
  isn't, push in once, slowly, rather than shrinking anything else.

## 4. Words and safety

| Use | Never use |
|---|---|
| check-in, checks in | notification, alert, reminder, ping, nudge |
| answer (Lighter, Still with it, Heavier) | rate, score, track your mood |
| write back, reply | therapist, counsellor, chat with a person |
| where you already are | everywhere, always on, 24/7 |
| more places soon | any other app's name or logo |

Don't put **spark**, **ember**, **lantern**, **kindling**, **outreach**, **channel** or
**delivery** on screen; they're names of other things in Xolace.

- **Gentle and hopeful, never clinical.** No graphs, no brain imagery.
- **No health claims.** Never say Xolace treats, cures, heals or fixes anything. The sky
  clears because the person answered Lighter, not because Xolace fixed something.
- **No drama.** No storms, lightning, rain or darkness. The heaviest weather is a soft
  lavender overcast. The clear sky is soft too: no cartoon sun, sun rays, lens flares or
  rainbows.
- **No nagging.** No piles of messages, red badges, counters or "missed" anything.
- **Telegram appears only as it really is**, in the footage. "Telegram" in plain type on
  the end card. No paper-plane logo unless approved assets are supplied.
- **Accessibility.** No flashes, text contrast of at least 4.5:1, every line held long
  enough to read twice.

## 5. Concept: "How's it sitting now?"

*Pitch this, sharpen it, or beat it (see §11, phase 1).*

**The idea in one line.** The sky is how you feel. It stays overcast for hours, until
Xolace checks in where you are, and one tap lets it clear.

The whole film is one continuous sky. It does three things, and only these:

| The sky does this | It means |
|---|---|
| Stays a soft lavender overcast, a glow hidden low behind it | Something from earlier is still sitting with you |
| Moves the hidden glow across in one slow arc, and shifts tone a little | Hours pass. Life carries on |
| Clears, top first, the moment the person taps Lighter | Being asked, and answering, helped a little |

The phone is the only object. It plays the real Telegram footage: the check-in arriving,
the tap, the reply.

## 6. Story: beat by beat (24 s)

## 7. Two ratios, two compositions

Design each ratio on its own; no centre crops. The phone is always whole, never touching
the frame edge.

| Beat | 16:9 landscape | 9:16 portrait |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 Sky | Full-frame sky. S1 and S2 centred, a little above the middle. | Full-frame sky. S1 and S2 centred in the key content zone. |
| 3–5 Phone | Phone about 82% of frame height, its centre at about 64% of frame width. Supers on the left, in the open sky, left-aligned, vertically centred on the phone. | Phone about 64% of frame height, centred horizontally, its top at about 26% of frame height. Supers above it, centred. |
| 6 End | End card centred on the clear sky. | End card centred in the key content zone. |

**The phone itself:** a simple, slim device silhouette in near-black (`#0B0A10`), rounded
to match the screen, with a soft, wide shadow (about 6% of the phone's height, 25%
opacity). No device-maker logos, no buttons, no reflections or glass glare.

## 8. Look: palette, type, sky and motion

### Palette

| Role | Colour | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Overcast sky | `#ECE7FF` at the top → `#D9D4E6` at the bottom | Beats 1–3 |
| Overcast cloud bands | `#C9C2DA` at 30–60% opacity | Beats 1–3 |
| Clear sky | `#7DD3E8` at the top → `#F7F5FB` in the middle → `#FFE2AE` at the horizon | From beat 4 |
| Xolace's glow | core `#FFE2AE`, edge `#F2A33A`, fading to clear | The light behind the cloud, then out in the clear sky |
| Ink | `#191524` | Supers, on light skies only |

### The sky, step by step

It's the only animated thing, so build it well:

1. A full-frame vertical gradient (the base).
2. A radial glow for the light, behind the cloud layer.
3. Two or three very wide, soft cloud bands: long horizontal ellipses with edges blurred
   heavily (about 100 px at 1080p), no outlines, drifting sideways at about 1% of frame
   width per second, each at a slightly different speed.
4. A light film grain (2–3%) over everything so the gradients don't band.

**Clearing (beat 4):** the base crossfades from overcast to clear, the cloud bands fade to
about 10% and drift apart out of frame, the glow brightens and grows about 10%. All over
about 3 s. No sun disc, rays or flares.

### Type

All free Google Fonts.

- **Newsreader Italic:** S1 to S3 and the end-card line.
- **Bricolage Grotesque Bold:** the end-card title only.
- **Figtree Medium:** the CTA and small print.

Sentence case. Supers appear and leave as a whole line: fade with a short rise (about 1.5%
of frame height), 0.32 s, cubic-bezier(.23, 1, .32, 1); fade out 0.2 s. No per-letter or
per-word animation, no scaling, no morphing.

### Motion language

- **Calm and continuous.** Long eased moves, every move ends in stillness, no hard cuts
  between beats.
- **Curves from the app:** arrive cubic-bezier(.33, 1, .68, 1); ease-out (.2, .8, .2, 1);
  settle (.23, 1, .32, 1). No overshoot or bounce.
- **The phone moves twice in the whole film:** in (beat 3) and out (beat 6).
- **No** glitches, whip pans, zoom blurs, light leaks, 3D flips or shape bursts.

### Using the footage

- Interface at 100% speed. Trim between moments; never speed up or ramp.
- Replace the status bar with a clean one (9:41, full signal and battery). Otherwise, add
  nothing to the interface and don't grade it.
- If a recording is missing, use the supplied screenshot of that state and change between
  states with a 0.2 s crossfade. Never rebuild or invent interface.

## 9. End card and CTA

Held at least 3 s, in the same layout as the Letters film.

1. Xolace logo lockup (supplied).
2. **Check-ins on Telegram** (Bricolage Grotesque Bold).
3. *Xolace checks in where you already are.* (Newsreader Italic)
4. CTA, two versions:
   - **A (default):** Coming soon to Xolace
   - **B (launch day):** Now in Xolace. Link Telegram in Settings → Reach me on…
5. Small line: *More places soon.*

## 10. Sound

- **No voiceover.** It must read with the sound off.
- **Music:** the Letters film's family: warm piano or soft Rhodes with a light pad, about
  75 bpm. Hushed under the overcast, opening up when the sky clears, resolving on the end
  card. No sad minor-key piano, heartbeats, ticking, choirs, risers or drops.
- **Sound design:** soft room tone, one warm message tone (never a buzz), a soft tap. That's
  all.
- **Mix:** -14 LUFS integrated, true peak no higher than -1 dBTP, stereo 48 kHz.
- **Rights:** original or fully licensed for worldwide, perpetual use on organic and paid
  social, web and app stores.

## 11. Safe zones, delivery and order

**Safe zones.**

- **16:9 (at 1920 × 1080):** title safe 96 px left and right, 54 px top and bottom; keep the
  bottom 130 px clear of supers.
- **9:16 (at 1080 × 1920):** no text or key action in the top 220 px, the bottom 440 px or
  the right 140 px; 64 px left margin. Key content zone: x 64–940, y 220–1480.

**Phase 1, test:** one 16:9 frame each of beats 1, 3 and 4, and a motion test of
beats 3–4 (about 9 s: the phone rising, the check-in arriving, the tap, the sky clearing)
using the real footage. **Share it before going further.**

**Phase 2, animatic:** both ratios at full length, temporary music, footage placed, supers
in position.

**Phase 3, final:**

- 16:9: 3840 × 2160 ProRes 422 HQ, and 1920 × 1080 H.264.
- 9:16: 2160 × 3840 ProRes 422 HQ, and 1080 × 1920 H.264.
- 30 fps, Rec. 709, stereo 48 kHz, -14 LUFS.
- End card A and B for each ratio; the optional 15 s cut; textless masters; music, effects
  and mix stems; an SRT per ratio; a cover frame per ratio; project files, font list and
  music licence.
