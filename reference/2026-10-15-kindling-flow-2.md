# Kindling: from clarity to support

The user is on Xolace+.

1. **Reflect.** The user writes what's on their mind.
2. **The mirror.** Xolace reflects it back in clearer words. The user taps **"That's it"**.
3. **Next step.** The user picks how to close the session. They can sit with it, see what others have shared, or just leave it there.
4. **Kindling announced.** Xolace says it's gathering a few things for them.
5. **Kindling is built.** In the background, Xolace picks 4 support steps for what the user shared. Each step comes with a short line on why it was picked.
6. **Notification.** "Your kindling is ready."
7. **Open the kindling.** The user opens it from the notification or from the card on the Discovery tab. They see a trail of 4 steps leading to Flux's campfire.
8. **Tend the steps.** The user does them in any order:
   - breathe (Sit with this)
   - listen (support audio or music)
   - read (Lantern)
   - talk to a Xolacer
   - reach out to someone they trust (Trusted Bridge)

   Each step they finish lights up the trail toward the fire. They can pass on any step that isn't for them.
9. **Done.** The kindling ends when every step is done or passed. The next session brings a new one.
